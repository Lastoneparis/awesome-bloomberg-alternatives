# Awesome Bloomberg Alternatives [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of free and affordable alternatives to the Bloomberg Terminal — for independent traders, quants, researchers, and developers.

The [Bloomberg Terminal](https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/products/bloomberg-terminal/) costs ~$24,000 per user per year. This list collects tools that replicate a subset of its functionality — market data, screeners, news, analytics, economic calendars, and on-chain data — for free or at a fraction of the cost.

## Full Terminals

All-in-one alternatives that try to replicate the Bloomberg Terminal experience.

- **[Swiss-Quant](https://swiss-quant.ch)** — Free financial terminal from Switzerland. AI forecasts for crypto/equity/forex/commodities, SEC insider tracker, on-chain analytics, economic calendar. No signup. *Free*.
- **[Koyfin](https://www.koyfin.com)** — Institutional-grade charting, fundamentals, news, macro data. Generous free tier. *Freemium*.
- **[QuantLink](https://www.quantlink.ai)** — AI equity research terminal for US stocks: deep-research agents grounded in SEC filings, a stock screener, institutional 13F holder analysis, insider (Form 4) activity, and congressional trade tracking. *Freemium*.
- **[TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com)** — The charting standard. Screeners, community scripts (Pine Script), alerts. *Freemium*.
- **[TIKR](https://www.tikr.com)** — Equity research platform with 10y+ financial data for 50k+ stocks globally. *Freemium*.
- **[Finviz](https://finviz.com)** — US equities screener and heatmaps. Basic but fast. *Freemium*.
- **[Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com)** — Clean stock data, fundamentals, SEC filings. *Free*.

## Market Data APIs

For building your own tools.

- **[Alpha Vantage](https://www.alphavantage.co)** — Stocks, forex, crypto, indicators. 25 requests/day free.
- **[Polygon.io](https://polygon.io)** — Institutional-grade equity data. Free tier limited.
- **[Finnhub](https://finnhub.io)** — Real-time stock quotes, news, fundamentals. 60 calls/min free.
- **[Twelve Data](https://twelvedata.com)** — Global market data across asset classes.
- **[Yahoo Finance API](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance)** (unofficial) — Python library, widely used.
- **[IEX Cloud](https://iexcloud.io)** — US equity data, cost-effective.
- **[Tiingo](https://api.tiingo.com)** — EOD + intraday stock data, crypto, news.

## Screeners & Scanners

- **[Finviz Screener](https://finviz.com/screener.ashx)** — The classic US equity screener.
- **[TradingView Screener](https://www.tradingview.com/screener/)** — Customizable across global markets.
- **[Swiss-Quant Screener](https://swiss-quant.ch)** — Free realtime screener with AI signals.
- **[StockTwits](https://stocktwits.com)** — Sentiment + social screener.

## Economic Data

- **[FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org)** — Federal Reserve Economic Data. 800k+ series, free API.
- **[World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org)** — Country-level macro indicators.
- **[OECD Data](https://data.oecd.org)** — Economic statistics for OECD members.
- **[Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com)** — Economic calendar + indicators.
- **[Investing.com Economic Calendar](https://www.investing.com/economic-calendar/)** — Free, granular.

## SEC Filings & Insider Tracking

- **[SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar)** — Official source. Free API.
- **[OpenInsider](http://openinsider.com)** — Form 4 filings, free.
- **[Swiss-Quant SEC Tracker](https://swiss-quant.ch)** — Insider trading + institutional holdings.
- **[Quiver Quant](https://www.quiverquant.com)** — Alt-data including congressional trades.

## On-Chain & Crypto Analytics

- **[Glassnode](https://glassnode.com)** — On-chain metrics. Free tier available.
- **[CryptoQuant](https://cryptoquant.com)** — Exchange flows, whale tracking.
- **[DefiLlama](https://defillama.com)** — TVL across all chains. Completely free.
- **[Dune Analytics](https://dune.com)** — SQL queries on blockchain data.
- **[Nansen](https://nansen.ai)** — Wallet labels, smart money tracking. Paid.
- **[Swiss-Quant On-Chain](https://swiss-quant.ch)** — Free whale + exchange flow dashboards.

## News & Sentiment

- **[Finimize](https://finimize.com)** — Daily financial news, plain English.
- **[Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com)** — Free financial news.
- **[GDELT Project](https://www.gdeltproject.org)** — Global news sentiment, free API.
- **[Marketaux](https://www.marketaux.com)** — Financial news API, free tier.

## Forecasting & AI

- **[Swiss-Quant AI Forecasts](https://swiss-quant.ch)** — Daily forecasts across asset classes.
- **[Numerai](https://numerai.com)** — Crowdsourced hedge fund, free data.
- **[Prophet (Meta)](https://facebook.github.io/prophet/)** — Time series forecasting library.
- **[Nixtla](https://github.com/Nixtla/neuralforecast)** — State-of-the-art neural forecasting.

## Open Source Libraries

- **[OpenBB Terminal](https://github.com/OpenBB-finance/OpenBBTerminal)** — Open-source Bloomberg alternative (CLI + web).
- **[QuantLib](https://www.quantlib.org)** — Quant finance library in C++/Python.
- **[Zipline](https://github.com/quantopian/zipline)** — Backtesting engine.
- **[Backtrader](https://www.backtrader.com)** — Python backtesting framework.
- **[yfinance](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance)** — Yahoo Finance Python API.
- **[ccxt](https://github.com/ccxt/ccxt)** — Unified crypto exchange API (100+ exchanges).
- **[TA-Lib](https://ta-lib.org)** — Technical analysis indicators, widely used.
